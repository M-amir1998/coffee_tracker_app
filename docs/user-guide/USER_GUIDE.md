# Coffee Tracker user guide

Coffee Tracker keeps the coffee money of your group fair and transparent:
who drank how much, who paid, what is in the cash box and who owes whom.
The app is available in English and Spanish (Settings → Appearance &
language).

## Getting started

1. **Create an account** with your email address and a password (at least 8
   characters, letters and digits).
2. **Confirm your email.** Open the link in the email, or type the code from
   the email into the app.
3. **Join your group** by opening the invitation link you received, or by
   entering the invitation code. You can also create a new group; you become
   its administrator.

You can belong to several groups and switch between them at the top of the
screen. Each group has its own balances and history.

## Your balance

The home screen shows your balance in the current group:

| You see | It means |
|---------|----------|
| **You owe 4.20** | your coffees cost more than you have paid so far |
| **The group owes you 3.00** | you have paid more than you drank (for example you bought beans) |
| **You're all settled** | nothing to do |

Items waiting for someone's confirmation are shown separately. They do not
change your balance until they are confirmed.

## Recording coffee

- Tap **+1 Coffee**. The app confirms it with a short animation and a
  vibration on phones.
- Tapped by mistake? Use **Undo** in the message that appears. You can undo
  your own coffees for a short time (15 minutes unless your group changed it);
  after that, ask an administrator.
- **Several** lets you add up to 20 coffees at once, for example when you
  forgot to record them earlier today.
- Each coffee costs the price that applied when you drank it. A price change
  never changes coffees you already had.
- **No connection?** The coffee is saved on your device and sent automatically
  when you are back online. A banner shows how many are waiting. If one cannot
  be recorded (for example it is more than 7 days old), it stays visible with
  **Retry** and **Discard**. If you sign out while coffees are waiting, the app
  tells you; they are sent the next time you sign in on that device.

## Paying and buying

Open **Money** on the home screen.

- **Money into the cash box**: put the cash in the box first, then record it.
  In most groups a treasurer confirms it; until then it does not count.
- **Coffee beans bought**: choose who paid.
  - *My own money*: you are credited what you paid. Example: you owe 6.00 and
    buy beans for 15.00, so the group now owes you 9.00.
  - *Cash box*: the money comes out of the box; nobody is credited.
- **Payment between members**: when you pay someone directly (cash, bank
  transfer). The person who received the money confirms it; if *you*
  received the money, choose "I received" and it counts immediately.
- **Reimbursement**: if the group owes you money, you can ask a treasurer to
  pay you from the cash box. It counts once the treasurer has paid you.

You can **cancel** something you submitted while it is still pending. If it
was **rejected** and you disagree, open it in the activity list and choose
**Dispute**; an administrator who is not involved decides.

## Activity

The **Activity** page lists everything that happened in the group, newest
first, with filters (type, status, member, dates, cycle). Open an entry to see its details,
who confirmed it and its full history. Nothing is ever deleted: a mistake is
corrected by a reversal or a correction, both visible in the list.

## Group and settling up

- **Group** shows every member's balance. On a computer the table can be
  sorted by any column; on a phone each member is a card.
- **Settle up** suggests the payments that bring everyone to zero: first
  reimbursements from the cash box, then payments between members, then
  deposits into the box. Money already on its way is taken into account. The
  suggestions are a short, practical plan, not necessarily the smallest
  possible number of payments. Each step still has to happen in real life and
  be recorded.

## Coffee Heroes

A friendly leaderboard for the week, month, cycle or all time:

- the podium: **Coffee Legend**, **Espresso Engine** and
  **Caffeine Commander**,
- the **Coffee Hero of the month**,
- your **streak** (days in a row with coffee; weekends don't break it),
- **achievements** such as First Sip, Early Bird, Night Owl, Coffee
  Marathon, Consistency Champion, Bean Guardian, Generous Contributor and
  Coffee Veteran.

Prefer not to take part? In **Settings**, turn off **Show me on Coffee
Heroes** (others no longer see your name and count) or **Show Coffee Heroes**
(hides the whole section for you).

## Analytics

Charts for a chosen period: coffees per day or week, by weekday and by hour,
the value of coffee consumed, the cash box over time, this cycle compared with
the previous one, participation, and the bean purchases. Everything is
calculated on the server from the same records as your balance.

## Notifications

The bell shows requests (for example "confirm that you received 5.00") and
updates (a deposit confirmed, a price change, a cycle closed). Items that need
your decision are also shown on the home screen. Choose which kinds you get
in **Settings → Notifications**. Notifications are shown in the app; this
version does not send push notifications or emails for them.

## For treasurers

- **Approvals**: confirm or reject deposits, purchases and reimbursement
  requests from other members. You never decide on your own submissions.
- Record cash that a member handed to you ("Cash handed to me by a member"),
  and coffees or purchases on behalf of members who cannot do it themselves.
- **Reimburse from the cash box**: hand over the cash first, then record it.
  The app refuses to pay out more than the box holds or more than the member
  is owed.
- **Count the cash**: enter what is physically in the box. If it differs from
  the records, explain the difference; a correction is recorded.

## For administrators

- **Members**: invitations (with expiry and number of uses), roles, removing
  members. A member who leaves keeps their history; their balance stays in the
  group until it is settled or corrected.
- **Price**: change the coffee price now or from a future date. Past coffees
  keep their price.
- **Cycles**: close the accounting cycle (for example every month). Balances
  are carried into the next cycle; the closed cycle stays available as a
  report and cannot be changed. You type the cycle's name to confirm.
- **Corrections**: reverse a transaction or correct a member's balance. Both
  need a reason, are permanent and appear in the audit log.
- **Exports**: CSV files for balances, coffee statistics, transactions and
  purchases.
- **Audit log** and **Ledger integrity**: every administrative action is
  recorded; the integrity check verifies that the accounts add up.
- **Group settings**: whether deposits need a treasurer's confirmation,
  whether purchases need approval, and the undo window for coffees.

## Keyboard shortcuts (computer and web)

| Keys | Action |
|------|--------|
| Alt+C | record a coffee |
| Alt+1 ... Alt+9 | go to a section |
| Alt+R | refresh |
| Alt+/ | show all shortcuts |

## Your account

In **Settings** you can change your display name, password, theme (system,
light, dark) and language. **Leave group** is possible when your
balance is settled. **Delete account** removes your login; your past
transactions stay in the group's history without your name, because other
people's balances depend on them.

## Questions

**My deposit doesn't count yet.** A treasurer has to confirm it first. You
can see it as pending on the home screen and in the activity list.

**I bought beans, why is my balance negative?** Negative means the group owes
you money. It goes back towards zero as you drink coffee, or a treasurer can
reimburse you from the box.

**I recorded a coffee by mistake an hour ago.** Ask an administrator to
correct it; the correction will be visible in the history.

**Why can't I leave the group?** Your balance has to be zero first. Use
Settle up to see how.
